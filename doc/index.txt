# Getting Started

Welcome to the Industrial documentation! This site provides comprehensive guides and reference materials for the Industrial system, a powerful platform for managing and deploying cloud-native applications. The Industrial system is designed to help you build, deploy, and manage applications in a cloud-native environment, with a focus on scalability, reliability, and ease of use.
